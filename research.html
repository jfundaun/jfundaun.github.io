Research

Traumatic head and neck injuries are a leading cause of chronic neuropathic pain. These injuries affect overlapping regions of the nervous system, from peripheral nerves and dorsal root ganglia through to the spinal cord and brain.

I study how injury to the nervous system across this spectrum leads to neuropathic pain, and why outcomes vary between patients. Using a deep phenotyping approach including MRI, quantitative sensory testing, neurological assessment, and blood-based measures. My ultimate aim is to better understand the mechanisms and underlying factors associated with chronic neuropathic pain after traumatic head and neck injury and to translate that understanding into better approaches to diagnosis and treatment.
